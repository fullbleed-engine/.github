# Fullbleed

**HTML/CSS to PDF for Python, Rust, Node.js, C#, and browsers.**

Fullbleed is an MIT-licensed Rust print engine for invoices, reports, statements,
letters, and other structured documents. Write HTML/CSS templates, supply your
fonts and assets, and generate PDFs in your application.

**[Try an invoice in your browser](https://docs.fullbleed.dev/assets/browser-demo/index.html)**
· [Documentation](https://docs.fullbleed.dev/)
· [Designed examples](https://docs.fullbleed.dev/examples/)

Change the customer name, invoice reference, or ink color, then select
**Generate PDF**. This trial runs locally without an account or installation.

To keep your edits as a runnable project, open the [PDF playground](https://docs.fullbleed.dev/playground/).
Under **Files and projects**, choose Python or Node.js, then **Download project**.
On a phone, select **Edit source** to edit HTML/CSS.

## Help improve Fullbleed

For setup, styling, or integration questions,
[ask in Q&A](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/discussions/new?category=q-a).
Include your language or framework and a small fictional example.

[Report a rendering issue](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
with a small HTML/CSS reproduction, improve a guide, or contribute an example.
The [contributing guide](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)
explains how to get started.
